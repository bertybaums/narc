Before you start

What you'll do
  1. You'll see a short sequence of colored grids that tell a story. You see all of them, including one grid the model won't see.
  2. An AI model ({{ s.model }}) gets every other grid, plus a narrative that you write. From those alone it has to rebuild the hidden grid exactly, cell for cell.
  3. Write a narrative that helps it do that, then test it. You'll see whether the model got it right. Revise and try again as many times as you like, whether or not your last try worked.

Every puzzle here can be solved this way: for each one we already have a narrative that led an AI model to the right answer. You can look at it whenever you like, but try on your own first. Once you've read it, it's hard to write something that isn't shaped by it.

What we record
  • Every narrative you submit, word for word, not only your last one.
  • When you submitted each one, and how long you spent writing it.
  • Whether you had looked at the existing narrative before you submitted.
  • The model's full response, and whether it solved the puzzle.

Stories you write may be published, word for word, on this site, in papers, and in public datasets, without your name or invite code.

We don't ask for your name or email. Your submissions are linked to a random ID kept in this browser and to the invite code, which is shared by everyone invited through the same event or mailing. Because of that, we cannot find or remove your stories after you submit them. Please do not put your name or other personal information in a story.
